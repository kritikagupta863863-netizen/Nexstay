NEXSTAY
The Operating System for India's PG Industry

Founder: Shikhar Agnihotri




Problem
India's Paying Guest (PG) industry serves over 20 million students and working professionals, yet most
properties are still managed using WhatsApp, Excel sheets, paper records, and manual follow-ups.


This leads to:


      • Missed rent collections
      • Poor occupancy management
      • Lost tenant records
      • Manual complaint handling
      • No business insights
      • Lack of transparency for tenants

For tenants, finding a trustworthy PG remains equally difficult due to fragmented listings, hidden charges,
and limited visibility into amenities and policies.




Solution
NEXSTAY is an AI-powered operating system built for PG accommodations.


Instead of using multiple disconnected tools, PG owners can manage their entire business from one
platform.


NEXSTAY combines:


• PG Management ERP


• Tenant Super App


• PG Marketplace


into one connected ecosystem.


Owners manage operations.

Why Now?
India's rental housing market is rapidly formalizing.


      • Increasing migration to Tier-1 & Tier-2 cities
      • Growing student population
      • Rising demand for organized co-living
      • Rapid digital adoption among SMBs
      • UPI-driven digital payments

Despite these trends, PG management software adoption remains extremely low, creating a significant
opportunity for a category-defining platform.




Market Opportunity
TAM (Total Addressable Market)

India's student housing and PG market is estimated at ₹50,000+ crore, serving over 2 crore residents.


SAM (Serviceable Available Market)

Over 100,000+ professionally managed PGs across India's major cities.


SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)

Capturing just 1% of the organized PG market (~1,000 PGs) creates a multi-crore recurring SaaS business.
