NEXSTAY
The Operating System for Modern PGs

NEXSTAY is a complete PG ecosystem platform that combines:


     • PG Management System
     • PG Marketplace
     • Tenant Living App

into one unified platform.


Instead of managing PG operations manually through WhatsApp, Excel sheets, paper invoices, and calls,
NEXSTAY digitizes the complete workflow for both PG owners and tenants.
